Biomechanical Defect Detection

PoseGuard AI combines deterministic kinematic bounds with temporal residual convolutions to isolate and categorize physical motion anomalies.

👟

1. Foot Sliding (Skating)

Occurs when an animation character's foot touches down on the ground plane but shifts or drifts horizontally relative to the environment without intentional locomotion traction.

Detection Criteria:
• Ground Contact State: Foot height \(Y_{foot} \le Y_{ground} + 0.045\text{ m}\)
• Horizontal Velocity: \(v_{xz} = \sqrt{v_x^2 + v_z^2} > 0.08\text{ m/s}\)
• Persistence: Sustained horizontal slip \(\ge 3\) consecutive frames
🕳️

2. Ground Penetration (Floor Clipping)

Occurs when the character's feet, ankles, or lower limb joints pass beneath the established ground reference plane, creating immersion-breaking visual artifacts in game engines or render viewports.

Detection Criteria:
• Vertical Joint Position: \(Y_{joint} < Y_{ground} - 0.02\text{ m}\)
• Severity Escalation: Measured in negative millimeters beneath the floor datum
• Support: Auto-calibrated ground level or manual animator override (\(--ground-y\))
⚡

3. Unnatural Pose Transitions (Jitter & Pops)

Flags sudden acceleration discontinuities, popping limbs, and unnatural angular velocities resulting from retargeting gimbal-lock flips, corrupted mocap frames, or dropped interpolation keys.

Detection Criteria:
• Joint Acceleration: \(a_{joint} = \left\|\frac{\mathbf{v}_{t} - \mathbf{v}_{t-1}}{\Delta t}\right\| > 90.0\text{ m/s}^2\)
• Jerk Discontinuity: Temporal spike exceeding natural biological joint limits
• Differentiation: Distinguishes intentional sprint bursts from instantaneous single-frame teleports
🔄

4. Contact Inconsistency (Chatter & Drag)

Detects conflicting foot flight states, such as rapid alternating ground contacts (chatter) or feet dragging along the floor during mid-swing without appropriate vertical clearance.

Detection Criteria:
• State Chatter: \(\ge 3\) transitions within any 5-frame temporal window
• Swing Clearance: Forward velocity \(v_z > 0.4\text{ m/s}\) while vertical clearance remains below \(0.015\text{ m}\)
📏

5. Bone Stretch Anomalies (Non-Rigid Rig Violations)

Enforces anatomical rigid-body distance conservation along skeletal limb segments (e.g. Femur, Tibia, Humerus).

Detection Criteria:
• Segment Length: \(L(t) = \|\mathbf{p}_{child}(t) - \mathbf{p}_{parent}(t)\|\)
• Stretch Threshold: \(\frac{|L(t) - L_{nominal}|}{L_{nominal}} > 0.06\) (6% tolerance)
• Rig Invariance: Preserves artistic scale factors while flagging unintentional IK rubber-banding
