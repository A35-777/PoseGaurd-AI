Privacy Notice & Guarantees

PoseGuard AI is built from the ground up to respect data sovereignty and studio confidentiality.

1. Complete Offline Execution

PoseGuard AI requires no internet connection to parse animation files, run neural inferences, or display the 3D viewport. All machine learning computations execute in-process via local CPU instructions.

2. Zero Telemetry & Zero Tracking

The software contains zero analytics trackers, telemetry collectors, diagnostic beacons, or user identification cookies. We do not track what files you inspect, which defects are discovered, or how often you launch the tool.

3. Local Data Storage & Cleanup

When running the local web server, temporary file uploads are written strictly to your operating system's standard temporary directory (`%TEMP%` on Windows, `/tmp` on Linux) and remain strictly inaccessible from outside your local loopback address (`127.0.0.1`).

4. Intellectual Property Protection

Your skeletal rigs, animation keyframes, and motion capture data are your intellectual property. Because PoseGuard AI never uploads or broadcasts data, it is safe for use under strict non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and commercial studio pipelines.
