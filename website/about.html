About PoseGuard AI

PoseGuard AI was conceived with a strict technical constraint: build a complete, highly effective animation quality inspection system capable of training and running on consumer hardware with zero cloud dependencies and zero budget.

Philosophy: Tiny AI Over Bloated Foundations

In an era where software routinely pulls multi-gigabyte foundation models or depends on high-latency cloud APIs, PoseGuard AI demonstrates what disciplined domain-specific modeling can accomplish.

Rather than treating 3D skeletal data as generic token sequences or RGB video streams, PoseGuardNet operates directly on 48 channels of invariant kinematic physics: joint velocities, second-derivative accelerations, relative ground heights, and bone segment rigidities. By embedding physical inductive biases directly into the feature representation, a 235,221 parameter network (941 KB) out-diagnoses massive unspecialized architectures while executing on CPU in under 0.75 milliseconds.

Independent Training & Zero Budget Engineering

PoseGuard AI was engineered, trained, and evaluated autonomously on an entry-level development workstation:

  • Workstation CPU: AMD PRO A12-9800B R7 (4 physical cores, 2.7 GHz base clock)
  • System RAM: 8 GB DDR4 (process memory strictly kept under 280 MB)
  • GPU Hardware: Integrated AMD Radeon graphics (Zero CUDA, zero cloud GPUs)
  • Budget: ₹0 / $0 — Zero paid APIs, zero commercial dataset licenses, zero cloud subscriptions

Public Beta & Honest Positioning

We do not claim that PoseGuard AI solves all character animation defects or eliminates the need for human artistic review. It is engineered as an automated QA assistant:

Defect reports are structured suggestions designed to accelerate review workflows, prevent common mocap retargeting flaws from slipping into game builds, and provide objective physical bounds for character locomotion.
