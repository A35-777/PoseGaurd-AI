Empirical Benchmark Evaluation

Rigorous comparative evaluation across deterministic, statistical, neural, and hybrid detection architectures.

⚠️ Evaluation Methodology & Data Split Caveat

The benchmark numbers reported below were measured on an unseen, held-out test split consisting of 84 temporal windows (40 frames each) from the Subject 6 (Pivot Turn) motion family. The training set strictly excluded all Subject 6 sequences to prevent sequence leakage. While this demonstrates rotational and temporal generalization across sharp turning kinematics, it should not be interpreted as empirical proof of universal generalization across all artistic styles, stylized cartoon rigs, or non-humanoid creatures.

Why Combine Neural Temporal Learning with Geometric Calibration?

Pure deterministic geometry suffers from severe false positive rates (38.75%) on non-linear motions such as sharp pivot turns, where centripetal foot acceleration mimics sliding artifacts. Conversely, unconstrained neural models can hallucinate defects in ambiguous temporal windows.

The PoseGuard AI Calibrated Hybrid Architecture gates neural defect probabilities against physical geometric boundary tests. If either subsystem flags an anomaly, it is categorized according to confidence level (Confirmed vs Suggested for Review), achieving high precision (92.18%) and an exceptionally low false positive rate (2.06%) on unseen kinematic turns.
